# How to connect to the taksiegra server

1) Register an account at: https://osu.taksiegra.ovh
2) Create a shortcut to the osu! .exe, then in the Properties field add: -devserver taksiegra.ovh
3) Launch osu! and log in with your registration username/password.
4) After a successful login, your account will automatically change from "Pending verification" to active.
